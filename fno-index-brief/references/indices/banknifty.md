# NIFTY BANK (Bank Nifty)

**Key:** `BANKNIFTY` · **Exchange:** NSE · **Strike step:** 100

## What it is
12 most liquid large banking stocks. More volatile than Nifty; very sensitive to rates, liquidity and bank results.

## Heavyweights & drivers to check
- HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank (together a very large share — these two often decide the day)
- SBI, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Axis Bank
- IndusInd Bank, Federal Bank, Bank of Baroda, AU Small Finance Bank, IDFC First, PNB, Canara

## News triggers
- **RBI**: repo rate, CRR, liquidity operations, regulatory actions on banks
- Bond yields, system liquidity, credit growth / deposit growth data
- Bank quarterly results: NII, NIM, asset quality (GNPA/NNPA), slippages
- Bank-specific events: management change, RBI penalties, M&A
- FII flows (banks are a large part of FII holdings)

## Relationship to other indices
Usually leads Nifty. If HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank diverge, Bank Nifty tends to range — mention it.
