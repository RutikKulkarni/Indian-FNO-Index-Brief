# BSE SENSEX

**Key:** `SENSEX` · **Exchange:** BSE · **Strike step:** 100

## What it is
BSE's 30-stock benchmark. Moves almost in line with Nifty 50 but has its own option chain and expiry on BSE.

## Heavyweights & drivers to check
- HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Reliance Industries, Infosys, Bharti Airtel, TCS,
  Larsen & Toubro, ITC, SBI, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank

## News triggers
- Same macro drivers as Nifty 50 (RBI, Fed, crude, FII flows, results)
- Thursday is Sensex expiry — expect pinning near max pain and fast moves in the
  last hour

## Relationship to other indices
~1:1 directional with Nifty 50; differences come from weights and its separate expiry-day positioning.

## Contract notes (verified 24 Oct 2026 — re-verify if older than 30 days)
- Exchange symbol: `SENSEX` (BSE)
- Lot size: 20
- Expiry: **weekly every Thursday**; monthly = last Thursday of the month
- Strike interval used for analysis: 100
- BSE's single weekly benchmark (Sept 2025). Option chain is on BSE, not NSE

- Holiday rule: if the expiry day is a trading holiday, expiry moves to the
  **previous trading day** (e.g. Nifty weekly 10 Nov 2026 → Mon 9 Nov; NSE
  monthly 24 Nov 2026 → Mon 23 Nov).

Lot sizes and expiry days change by SEBI / exchange circular. Verify them with
sources dated in the **last 30 days**; if a newer circular exists, use it and
mention the change in the brief.
