# NIFTY 50

**Key:** `NIFTY` · **Exchange:** NSE · **Strike step:** 50

## What it is
India's benchmark: 50 large caps across sectors. The broadest read of the market and the most liquid index option in India.

## Heavyweights & drivers to check
- Financials: HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Axis Bank, Bajaj Finance
- Energy / conglomerates: Reliance Industries, Larsen & Toubro
- IT: Infosys, TCS, HCL Tech
- Consumer / others: ITC, Bharti Airtel, Hindustan Unilever, Mahindra & Mahindra

## News triggers
- RBI policy, inflation (CPI/WPI), GDP, fiscal announcements
- US Fed / US CPI / US bond yields (FII flows), dollar index
- Crude oil (inflation, Reliance, OMCs), USD/INR
- Results season: heavyweights' quarterly numbers
- Geopolitics, elections, budget

## Relationship to other indices
Moves with Bank Nifty on most days (financials are the largest weight). IT-led days can split Nifty from Bank Nifty — say so when it happens.

## Contract notes (verified 24 Oct 2026 — re-verify if older than 30 days)
- Exchange symbol: `NIFTY`
- Lot size: 65 (from Jan 2026 contracts)
- Expiry: **weekly every Tuesday**; monthly = last Tuesday of the month
- Strike interval used for analysis: 50
- Only NSE index with weekly options (NSE chose Nifty as its single weekly benchmark, Sept 2025)

- Holiday rule: if the expiry day is a trading holiday, expiry moves to the
  **previous trading day** (e.g. Nifty weekly 10 Nov 2026 → Mon 9 Nov; NSE
  monthly 24 Nov 2026 → Mon 23 Nov).

Lot sizes and expiry days change by SEBI / exchange circular. Verify them with
sources dated in the **last 30 days**; if a newer circular exists, use it and
mention the change in the brief.
