# NIFTY MIDCAP SELECT (Midcap Nifty)

**Key:** `MIDCPNIFTY` · **Exchange:** NSE · **Strike step:** 25

## What it is
25 liquid midcap stocks. Higher beta than Nifty; driven by domestic flows and risk appetite.

## Heavyweights & drivers to check
- Check the current constituent list on NSE before naming stocks — it is
  rebalanced periodically. Typically includes midcap names in capital goods,
  financials, auto ancillaries, IT services and consumer.

## News triggers
- Domestic retail / mutual fund flows (SIP data), small/midcap valuation commentary
- SEBI actions on mid/small caps, stock-specific results
- Broader risk-on / risk-off days (midcaps fall harder on sell-offs)

## Relationship to other indices
Higher beta than Nifty; a weak Nifty day is usually a weaker Midcap day.

## Contract notes (as of Oct 2026 — verify if stale)
- Exchange symbol: `MIDCPNIFTY`
- Lot size: 120 (from Jan 2026 contracts)
- Expiry: **monthly only** — last Tuesday of the month (weekly discontinued)
- Strike interval used for analysis: 25
- Thin option liquidity — say so; signals from OI are less reliable here

Lot sizes and expiry days change by SEBI / exchange circular. If a search shows
