# BSE BANKEX

**Key:** `BANKEX` · **Exchange:** BSE · **Strike step:** 100

## What it is
BSE's banking index. Similar constituents to Bank Nifty.

## Heavyweights & drivers to check
- HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Axis Bank, IndusInd Bank,
  plus smaller private and PSU banks

## News triggers
- Same as Bank Nifty: RBI, liquidity, bond yields, bank results, asset quality

## Relationship to other indices
Follows Bank Nifty almost tick-for-tick; use Bank Nifty's read as a cross-check.

## Contract notes (verified 24 Oct 2026 — re-verify if older than 30 days)
- Exchange symbol: `BANKEX` (BSE)
- Lot size: 30 (Oct–Dec 2026 contracts)
- Expiry: **monthly only** — last Thursday of the month (weekly discontinued Sept 2025)
- Strike interval used for analysis: 100
- Very low option liquidity — treat OI signals as weak and say so

- Holiday rule: if the expiry day is a trading holiday, expiry moves to the
  **previous trading day** (e.g. Nifty weekly 10 Nov 2026 → Mon 9 Nov; NSE
  monthly 24 Nov 2026 → Mon 23 Nov).

Lot sizes and expiry days change by SEBI / exchange circular. Verify them with
sources dated in the **last 30 days**; if a newer circular exists, use it and
mention the change in the brief.
