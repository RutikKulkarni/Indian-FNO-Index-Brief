# NIFTY NEXT 50

**Key:** `NIFTYNXT50` · **Exchange:** NSE · **Strike step:** 100

## What it is
The 50 companies ranked just after the Nifty 50 — large caps that could enter Nifty next. Includes PSUs and new-age companies.

## Heavyweights & drivers to check
- Check the current constituent list on NSE before naming stocks. Typically
  heavy in PSU banks/financials, power and defence PSUs, FMCG, and
  newer-listed large caps.

## News triggers
- PSU / government policy news (divestment, defence orders, power sector)
- Index rebalancing announcements (entries/exits from Nifty 50)
- Stock-specific results

## Relationship to other indices
Between Nifty and Midcap in beta; PSU-heavy days can split it from Nifty.
